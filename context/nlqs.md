# Business questions the model must answer

Source: the 18 brief NLQs of the Continental Bio build (ES Consulting hackathon, 2026-10-21).
Only the questions are given here; definitions of the business terms are inferred from the data.

1. What is our cash runway right now? (And what was it before the sale?)
2. How is spend tracking against budget by program, year to date?
3. Which program is most over budget?
4. When is the next CB-201 batch at Harbor Biologics, and is it on schedule?
5. How much GSK milestone value is still outstanding, and how much is pending GSK confirmation?
6. How much milestone value have we earned?
7. What have TSA support costs been to date, and how much does GSK reimburse?
8. What has attrition looked like since the sale, by department?
9. How is CB-201 enrollment tracking against target, by site?
10. How much is each owner (budget holder) spending this year?
11. How is net burn moving month over month?
12. How did we do against budget last quarter, by cost category?
13. How many doses do we need per month at current enrollment, and at +20% patients?
14. How many months of dose supply does each site have on hand?
15. How many batches do we need over the next 12 months at +20%, vs batches scheduled at the CDMO?
16. Which raw material runs out first under +20%?
17. What is the cold-chain temperature excursion rate by site?
18. What is the lead time to add a new trial site?
